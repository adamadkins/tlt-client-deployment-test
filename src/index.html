A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Curious? Us too.

Here are a few quick answers about this example storefront.

Can I place an order here?+

Yes, for items in the print shop. Payment is handled by Stripe and Printful prints and ships the order. The mittens are fictional and can't be ordered.

Are these mittens available for purchase?+

No. The mittens, their prices and inventory are fictional. The print shop above is the real shop.

What's this website testing?+

The Little Things Studios is testing how to build a website under the studio account, get client approval, and publish to a Cloudflare Worker owned by the client.
